## Artwork and archive integration

rule. Their source centers and filtered alpha-8 bounds fit the native runtime
canvases. Low-alpha resampling fringe can still extend beyond the canvas;
this is not a claim that every alpha-1 pixel survives cropping. The two final
foot-contour edits retain their raw generated bytes and exact prompts. Small
redraw differences elsewhere are not described as pixel-exact foot-only edits.

## Metadata decisions and review

The full catalog validates inherited approval chains and each current asset's
actual originating review. Selected-subset inheritance permits future asset
replacement while retaining validation of the complete historical chain.
Duplicate coverage, cycles, changed raw hashes and mismatched recipes are
refused. The existing pilot metadata stays scoped to its original 21 assets;
new families do not silently expand its supplied-original import set.

Independent review found malformed active-approval and inheritance structures
could raise unhelpful Python type errors. Shape checks now provide named
diagnostics; executed guard-removal mutations verify those paths. A future
replacement of an original pilot asset still needs an explicit pilot-history
transition, as recorded in [BACKLOG.md](../BACKLOG.md).

Two earlier reproduction runs overlapped finalization of an acceptance record
and correctly detected changed inputs. After the record was frozen, the
outputs were regenerated and all final smoke, regression, mutation and
reproduction checks passed. No runtime failure is attributed to that
authoring overlap.
