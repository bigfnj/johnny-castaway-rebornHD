Frame 003 is shared by walking and ordinary turns. Its 19 route-table rows
across 11 groups and 12 ordinary-turn rows make isolated promotion unsuitable.
The full eight-frame profile family is the delivery unit. Ordinary 009, 010
and 012 remain later route/story batches; this work does not approve them.

Original decoded geometry determines pose, occlusion and foot roll. Approved
Cartoon standing 000 determines identity, expression, cap, beard, shorts and
drawing style. Diagnostic original colors and gray ground shadows are not
the intended palette or sole landmarks. No anatomical left/right claim is
inferred from screen-left/screen-right alone.

Use the established 0.1 drawing scale and premultiplied export filters, with
original-derived runtime canvases and explicit cap targets. Do not independently
fit or stretch each pose, alter the engine route, trim meaningful art, or add
interpolated steps. A candidate that overhangs stays draft-only until corrected.

Keep exact prompts, actual ordered input paths, preserved hashes, generated
ancestors, raw output bytes, measurements and scoped human decisions. Static
pose approval does not constitute gait or runtime acceptance. Native tests run
without taking over the user's active desktop.
