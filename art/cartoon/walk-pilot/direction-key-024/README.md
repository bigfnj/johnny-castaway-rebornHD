# Frame 024 directional key: direction approved

Revisions 4, 5 and 6 are separate directional key candidates. After a review
explanation explicitly limited to revision 6's body direction and leg order,
the user replied "well done". This is recorded as approval of those two aspects.
None is a final registered runtime frame or an accepted walking cycle. Rear-foot
height was excluded from the check and requires further refinement and review.

Revision 4 starts from the original frame enlarged with nearest-neighbor
sampling and the approved character appearance sheet. It does not use the
earlier side-profile walking drawings. Revision 5 edits revision 4, and
revision 6 edits revision 5; both also use the same enlarged original pose.
The three exact prompts and confirmed ordered reference lists are recorded
in `provenance.json`.

`source-images.zip` contains only the three generated PNGs, unchanged. The
original pose remains in the production archive and the character sheet is
already tracked. Their hashes are recorded; no external cap references are
included. The enlarged pose reference is a technical 12x nearest-neighbor
copy of the original RGBA pixels and can be reconstructed from that source.

The stored still-preview transform uses uniform scale 0.1 and cap translation
(-21, -3.95), on a 64-by-150 canvas. It maps raw cap landmark (385, 42) to
(17.5, 0.25). This transform belongs to the revision-6 direction comparison
only. It is not the prior six-frame export scale or a final runtime
registration; future motion frames require a documented common scale and
fresh review. No bounding-box fitting or limb deformation was used in this
technical preview.

Generated transparency is retained exactly, including RGB values under zero
alpha. The preview's alpha composite resolves the apparent backdrop visible
in the raw image display; no background removal was applied. Prompts do not
reproduce image generation: preserved PNG bytes and hashes identify these
specific outputs, and no seed or model identifier was exposed.

These records do not modify the previous six-pose recipe, exporter, runtime
archive or story scripts.
