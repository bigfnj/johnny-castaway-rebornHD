Preserve the lean body, expressive eyes, long rounded nose, shaggy dark hair,
sunburned skin, and ragged white shorts. The beard is full, slightly long and
scruffy. The white sailor cap has a projecting dark brim, narrow gold band and
small anchor emblem. Earlier versions with a flat cap or an upturned bowl rim
were rejected; do not use them as character references.

The original game frames remain the authority for each animation pose, canvas
size, padding, foot position, prop contact and timing. The concept sheet supplies
the character identity and drawing style. The first motion test must be reviewed
at actual playback scale before expanding production.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The exact accepted revision prompt
is recorded in `character-v5-prompt.txt`. The tool did not expose a reproducible
seed or model identifier. Accepted pixels, their hashes and human review are the
production record.
