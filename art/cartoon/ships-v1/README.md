# SHIPS family draft

This is a visual art draft for the complete 21-slot SHIPS.BMP resource. Exact originals, current HD proxies, diagnostic guides and grouping constraints are documented in `reference/source.json` and `preflight.md`. Nothing in this bundle is promoted to the runtime package.

Frames000 through002 are labeled "Small source shape; role unconfirmed". Their forked outlines and light bands are retained, but neither distant-vessel identity nor a hand/arm identity is established. The saved map has no unique-slot action attribution for them.

For subgroup 000 through 013, select `generation/004-generated-v2.png` and `005-generated-v2.png`, and the v1 files for000 through 003 and 006 through 009. `generation/record-000-013.json` binds those ten selected drawings and every executed request/raw version. Exact prompts, ordered image-reference hashes, actual output dimensions, alpha bounds and raw-output hashes are retained in each matching per-frame record.

Frames 010 through 013 are four unchanged source slots in `source-preserved/`: each original 8 by 1 canvas has one opaque black pixel and seven transparent pixels. Their role remains unproven. Keeping them does not assert they are unused or establish a placeholder role. No new drawing was invented for these slots.

The complete 008 ship established the brown wood, gold trim, ivory sails and turquoise water identity. Frame 003 then established the lower-strip identity for 004 through 006. The first 004 and 005 drafts picked up too much diagnostic cobalt blue; one targeted built-in edit per frame restored turquoise water in v2. Those earlier raws remain unchanged. The edit request was color-only, but generated output is not asserted to preserve all other pixels exactly.

All selected 000 through 009 raws have no pixels with alpha at least 8 on their outer canvas edges. Their actual canvases vary from the requested dimensions: 000 through 002 and 007 are 1536 by 1024,003 is 2171 by 724,004 through 006 are 2172 by 724, and008/009 are 1254 by 1254. All retain their original generated RGBA bytes. No threshold, crop, color key or postprocessing was applied.

The dramatic brown halo and bottom matte in 007's raw tool preview disappear under actual alpha compositing. A neutral inspection is retained in ignored `build/ships-v1/007-neutral-inspection.jpg`; example bottom-center raw RGBA is 123,72,19,0. Faint alpha residue remains preserved. Alpha8 is an inspection diagnostic, not a mask applied to the image.

Registration remains open. In particular, the generated lower hulls have a fuller vertical proportion than the very shallow original strips, and 007 must meet them at their actual scripted positions. Visual family similarity does not prove pixel-exact joins, unchanged scale, native waterline contact or animation continuity. Those checks are deferred with export/native work under the art-first workflow.

The sibling effects subgroup 014 through 020 has its own generation records. The shared review should include those effects and the four source-preserved slots alongside these vessel drawings. No tests, production archive edits, runtime edits or commits were performed here.
