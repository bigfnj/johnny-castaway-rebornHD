The 26 new source slots are GJGULL1A.BMP 026-038, GJGULL3.BMP 017-024, and GJGULL3A.BMP 042-046. Earlier GJGULL1A 000-025 aliases of previously drafted gulls were excluded. GJANGRY 026-038 contains identical source aliases of this book sequence and was not counted twice.

Only light source/output visual review and metadata readback were performed. These are appearance candidates; final runtime size, registration, motion and production acceptance are still pending.
