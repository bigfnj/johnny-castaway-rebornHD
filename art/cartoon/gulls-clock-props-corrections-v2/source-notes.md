The authoritative correction list and exact user wording are preserved in feedback.json with the earlier review and annotation hashes. The 24 row identities are keyed as RESOURCE.BMP-NNN rather than by frame number alone.

GJGULL1A 026-030 carry the full-size book in the beak, with the upper bill over the book edge and lower bill occluded. Frames 031 and 033-038 concern a single full-size book, page damage/ruffling, and the exact leg or foot visibility stated in each row. Frame 032 is outside this correction request.

Original sprites retain their exact decoded pixels and diagnostic palette. Earlier Cartoon rows point to the precise versions recorded in feedback.json, including previous revisions. Corrected rows resolve the selected generation record and bind its request, output, ordered references and original tool-cache path. No original, earlier drawing or new raw is repainted or resampled by the gallery.

The canvas view fits each image independently to its alpha8 bounds using one uniform display scale, then draws the complete image with its existing alpha. It is an appearance comparison, not a demonstration of final scene registration, draw order, timing or native animation. Those checks remain for integration.
