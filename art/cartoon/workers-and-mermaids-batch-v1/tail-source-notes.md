# SMGFTWAV tail phase observations

Nine distinct originals show a green mermaid tail emerging, folding and disappearing. These are tail-only sprites: never add a head, torso, hands or the underwater continuation. Fin lobes remain one anatomical fluke even when the waterline hides their connection. White source strokes are attached water ripples. They are not a ground shadow.

000: Nearly submerged tail: only TWO small separate green fin-tip slivers emerge at the waterline, slightly left and right of center. The right tip is taller and more upright; left tip low and hooked. Keep their underwater connection hidden. White pale-cyan broken ripple arcs spread much wider than the tips to both sides. Extremely low wide silhouette. Do not draw a whole tail above the water.

001: Two green tail-fluke lobes rise separately above water, with a gap between them: left lobe low and curling left, right lobe taller pointing upward and right. The connecting middle remains underwater/occluded. White pale-cyan shallow curved water crescent beneath. Two unequal visible fin shapes, not legs or boots.

002: A single large green forked tail fluke emerges as a broad V. Both tips sweep upward/outward; image-right lobe bends more steeply up, image-left lobe spreads lower and farther left. A short central tapered stem points down into a small attached white/cyan ripple. Preserve this asymmetric V and original aspect ratio, no body above or below.

003: One green fluke folded strongly forward/down. The broader image-left lobe bends downward into a hook while the narrower image-right lobe extends right and curls down. Central root stays low above a small white/cyan ripple beneath. Compact curled silhouette with overlapping surfaces, not a spread upright V. One continuous forked fin with two lobes.

004: Tail viewed obliquely, sweeping up from a slender base at lower image-left and curving diagonally toward upper-right. Two forked lobes spread across the upper-right, with curled tips. Source shows a long C-like curve and much of the left side is empty. Small white/cyan splash only at lower-left base. Match that diagonal side view, do not replace it with a centered upright fork.

005: Upright tail-fluke root extends vertically down at center into white/cyan ripples. Broad fluke above spreads nearly horizontally: image-left lobe points outward left, image-right lobe curls upward into a rounder tip. Central notch shallow. Preserve its wide asymmetrical outline and straight downward root.

006: Broad asymmetrical V-shaped green fluke: image-left lobe curves upward, image-right lobe stretches farther right and up, central stem points down slightly right of center into a small white/cyan ripple. Fluke face partly turned relative to005. Keep exact lobe reach and pointed low root.

007: Compact green tail fluke folded toward the viewer into a rounded heart-like bulge, with its two tips curled inward across the top. Narrow root points down into small broken white/cyan ripples. Foreshortened front view, not a spread side-to-side fan. Preserve the central notch and overlapping curled lobes.

008: Final near-submerged low silhouette: just a thin horizontal green fin ridge with two small rounded humps/tips at water level; no tall stem. Tiny white/cyan broken ripples encircle lower edge and extend both sides. Wide low profile, even lower than000. Hide the rest underwater.

Use the selected SBREAKUP tail material as identity reference only. Each exact SMGFTWAV original controls orientation, exposed area and fold direction. Consecutive frame numbers are a still-reference order, not verified native timing.

Final appearance selections use version 2 for 001, 005, 006 and 008, and version 1 otherwise. 001 reduces the initially excessive exposed fin height. 008 restores the connected low ridge with blunt humps.
