# 36 marine scene drawings

This set contains 17 octopus poses from GJCATCH2, 12 animal diving judges from GJDIVE and seven SPLASH effects. It follows approval of the ten gull and clothing corrections.

Open [review.html](review.html) to compare each Cartoon drawing with its original. The gallery includes a frame filter and light/dark checkerboards. The judges hold blank scorecards: their numbers are separate source overlays, outside this drawing set.

[Source records](reference/source.json) preserve the exact archived originals and nearest-neighbor inspection copies. [The batch plan](batch-plan.json) records the selected slots and scope. Original geometry controls each pose; shared generated keys guide color and drawing style. The round octopus keeps the original eye visibility and the number of visible fish carried by its tentacles.

All artwork and exact prompts are in [generation](generation). Every `NNN-generated-vN.png` is an untouched built-in `image_gen.imagegen` output. Its `NNN-vN-request.json` saves the complete prompt and references; `NNN-record-vN.json` binds those files to the returned PNG. [Selected versions](selected-versions.json) identifies any revisions.

Appearance review is pending. This page enlarges drawings independently and does not establish animation timing. Native sizing, registration, score overlays and bulk smoke/regression checks follow during integration. The production archive is unchanged.

Selected revisions remove an invented head crest from early octopus drafts, smooth the crab's contours and correct the profile fish's eye count. GJCATCH2 038 retains three clearly visible fish heads and two exposed tails. Its first prompt hypothesized four visible fish, but closer source inspection found that the far-left green fragment is a tail-like form without a separate eye; the selected drawing preserves that occlusion instead of adding another face.
