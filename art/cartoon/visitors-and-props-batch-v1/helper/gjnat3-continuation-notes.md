# GJNAT3 continuation draft handoff

All fifteen assigned frames are ready: 019-031, 050 and 051. Recommended selection is version 2 for 031 and version 1 for the other fourteen. The original 031 version 1 remains preserved.

Each exact original nearest8 source and the shared GJNAT1 frame 005 key were inspected before request creation. Source pose notes are in gjnat3-continuation-poses.json. Each request was saved before its built-in ImageGen call; all sixteen raw results are copied byte-for-byte with their request/reference/output hashes and alpha measurements.

The drafts preserve side-to-back turns, the alternating raised legs and separate raised-arm phases. Rear poses show hair and back rather than a frontal face. 050 and 051 retain their different horizontal and downward open-arm poses. The source red/white skin stippling is interpreted as plain tan skin; gray source ground shadows are omitted. Frame 031 v1 held the mask too far out and nearly upright. V2 brings its broad face beside the torso, with the point down-left, and bends the carrying arm.

All selected PNGs are RGBA with transparent pixels. Meaningful alpha bounds remain inside their canvases. Maximum edge alpha is 0 or 1. Frame 029 has maximum alpha 254, and its apparent raw-viewer brown/green halo disappears in actual-alpha compositing. No alpha thresholding, cleanup, recoloring or other pixel edits were applied.

Actual-alpha source/draft contact sheets were inspected under build/visitors-continuation/contact-1.png through contact-3.png. The accompanying scratch summary.json lists selected output hashes and alpha measurements. The diagnostic helper gjnat3_continuation_inspect.py only composites those copies for inspection; it does not alter generated art.

These are human-review drafts. Registration, native timing, motion and production integration remain deferred; no full tests or approval claims are made.
